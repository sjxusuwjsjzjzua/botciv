# Prompts for concept pictures of the towns (for an image model)

Each prompt describes one of the plans drawn in this folder (`1_river.png` ... `5_forest.png`, to scale: the 100 m bar),
in the viewer's art direction (docs/viewer.md §4). Paste the shared style first, then one scene. Ask for 16:9.

## The shared style (paste before each scene)

> Cozy stylised 3D video-game render, low-poly but soft and rounded (bevelled edges, chunky proportions), toon shading
> with three soft light steps and a gentle dark outline on buildings and people, warm earthy palette, in the spirit of
> Stardew Valley, A Short Hike and Animal Crossing, but seen from a high three-quarter isometric camera about 120 m up,
> looking down at 45 degrees, so the whole village and its lanes are readable like a map. Small chibi people (big
> heads, round bodies) about the size of a door, going about their work. No text, no UI, no modern objects. Prehistoric
> to early bronze age technology: timber, wattle and daub, thatch, hides, felt, stone; no glass windows, no metal roofs.

## 1. River folk: a street village (late summer afternoon)

> A street village beside a wide slow river. One curving packed-earth lane, 12 m wide, runs along the river at about
> 200 m from the bank. On both sides of the lane, neat rectangular fenced plots (tofts) about 32 m wide and 56 m deep,
> wattle fences between them; in each plot one long house 12 m wide and 32 m long, gable end facing the lane, thick
> golden thatch down almost to the ground, a door in the gable; behind each house a small timber store on stilts and
> some plots with a little sheep pen. In the middle of the village the lane widens around a village green of short
> grass with a stone well and a small timber shrine; children play there. A narrow lane leads from the green down to a
> ford in the river. At the east (downwind) end of the village, three clay pottery kilns with thin smoke. Outside the
> west end, a fenced burial ground with rows of small upright stones. South of the village, long narrow strips of
> field, alternating ripe golden grain, green flax and brown fallow, with people reaping. Warm golden afternoon light.

## 2. Shore folk: a waterfront row (morning, light mist on the water)

> A fishing settlement on a sheltered sea bay. A single row of about 18 long houses stands along a sandy shore, each
> facing the water, dark weathered timber walls and dark thatched roofs, 12 m wide by 28 m deep, small yards between
> them with wooden fish-drying racks hung with fish. A sand-and-shell lane runs along the front of the houses, parallel
> to the water. Wooden jetties poke out from the beach every few houses with small hide boats and dugout canoes tied
> to them and pulled up on the sand. Behind the row, a small green with a well, a footpath winding inland past a fenced
> burial ground, and a patch of small striped fields. Calm turquoise water, gulls, morning mist lifting.

## 3. Hill clans: round houses inside a bank (autumn evening)

> A hilltop settlement on rolling upland grass. A great circular earthen bank with a ditch outside it, about 400 m
> across, encloses the settlement; three narrow ways cut through the bank. Inside, two concentric rings of round houses
> (16 m across, low stone walls, tall conical thatched roofs with smoke rising through the thatch) face packed-earth
> ring lanes. In the very centre a circular stone-walled sheep fold full of sheep. Small round sheep pens of dry stone
> beside many doors. Outside the bank, scattered oak and hawthorn trees, and on the slope below a group of stone cairns.
> One small pottery kiln outside the bank, smoking. People bringing sheep in for the night. Amber evening light, ochre
> and red autumn leaves.

## 4. Horse riders: a ring of tents (summer, late morning)

> A nomad camp on open rolling steppe grass, no trees, no fences, no fields. In the centre a large circular horse fold
> about 65 m across, a ring of rope and stakes, full of small sturdy horses. Around it two rings of round felt tents
> (yurts, about 7 m across, cream and white felt with red and ochre bands, low domed roofs, a smoke hole at the top),
> every tent's door facing the horse fold. A worn trodden ring path between fold and tents. Riders coming in along two
> faint tracks across the grass. A cooking fire outside the rings. A few low burial mounds in the distance. Big sky
> with soft white clouds, bright summer light.

## 5. Forest folk: huts in a clearing (spring, dawn)

> A small settlement in a clearing of an old broadleaf forest. In the middle a round fire-green with a low stone hearth
> and a fire burning. About 16 conical huts (12 m across, timber poles, bark and moss roofs, a hide over the door)
> stand scattered among tall trees left standing between them, each joined to the fire-green by a narrow winding
> footpath, the paths branching like a tree. Herb-drying racks beside some huts. Under great oaks at the edge, a few
> graves with wooden markers. Spring blossom, fresh green leaves, dawn rose light, smoke and mist hanging in the trees.

## 6. Close-up: one river-folk household (to show the scale of people)

> Ground-level view, camera 10 m up, of one household's plot in the river folk's street village: the wattle fence and
> gate on the lane, the long house's gable end with its door, a woman spinning on a bench by the door, a man carrying
> sheaves on his back up the lane, a child feeding geese, the small store on stilts and a sheep pen behind, the
> neighbours' long houses on both sides, the village green with its well visible down the lane. Same cozy toon style.

## 7. Night: the same street village

> The river folk's street village from prompt 1 at night in winter: snow on the thatch and the lane, blue moonlight,
> every long house's door glowing warm orange from the fire inside, smoke rising straight up, a few people with
> torches crossing the green to the well, the river frozen at its edges. Nobody wandering the fields: everyone is home.

## 8. Today, for contrast (optional)

> The same cozy toon style, high isometric view, of a chaotic sprawl: hundreds of tiny huts, pens, stores, kilns,
> fields and stone cairns packed edge to edge in a dense patchwork with no lanes and no open ground, people walking
> across roofs and fields in every direction. (This is how world4's settlements are laid out now.)
