# civ: the second generation

Status: **being built, from 2026-09-30.** The owner said: "Treat what we've built so far as
lessons learned and a trial, not as a foundation to build off of. Build big and ambitious." They
also asked for general bots, fitting the open-ended idea, to fill most of a world that also has
AI people in it.

`botciv/` (v1) keeps the live world running until `civ/` (v2) is proven. After that, the
worlds move to v2.

## Lessons from v1

1. **The engine owns the world; minds only choose.** Kept, and applied more strictly.
2. **Decisions are the budget.** v1 asked a mind at almost every hour anything happened, about
   2.4 times a person a day, and each hour of work was one step.
   - v2 minds choose **intentions**: a goal and a plan of steps, plus routines. An executor
     shared by every mind carries them out: walking, finding the nearest source, repeating
     work, loading a kiln, going home at dusk.
   - AI people are woken for choices, not for steps. The target is 1-2 decisions a day.
3. **Hidden recipes starve the story.** In 436 days, people chose to craft 52 times. v2 uses the
   known tree: everything is visible, and knowing how is skill learned by practice or teaching.
4. **Hard-coded kinds do not scale.** v1 had about 120 checks on building kind. In v2, content
   is data: items carry hooks, and buildings carry roles. The engine knows hooks and roles,
   never names.
5. **Prompts grow with the world** unless perception is summarised. v2 lists the nearest
   people, and far people as counts by direction. It shows crafts within reach, not the whole
   tree. A test holds the 95th-percentile prompt to a budget.
6. **Bots first.** A content tier is real once bots can climb it. v1's bots were hand-written
   ladders; v2's bots plan any goal through the recipe tree.
7. **Scarcity makes stories.** Easy food removed v1's struggle, but abundance with nothing to do
   is dull. In v2 each era brings new scarcities to trade, hire and fight over: tin, iron skill,
   land fit for a plough, labour, literacy.

## Mixed worlds: many bot people, some AI people

- **Scale:** a land of about 96×96 with 150-300 people. **AI people** (a language model's mind)
  are a minority, sized to capacity, perhaps 20-60 on the Kaggle GPU. The rest are **bot
  people**.
- **Bot people are people.** They have:
  - the same body, skills, memory of others, and possessions as AI people;
  - the same actions, through the same plan API;
  - personalities (industry, sociability, boldness, generosity, curiosity, ambition) and a
    vocation they drift toward by aptitude and demand: farmer, herder, potter, smith, trader,
    headman.

  They remember who wronged or helped them, answer proposals by trust, trade at posted prices,
  teach their children and apprentices, join groups, and follow group rules and leaders. They
  speak only a little, in short lines. Nothing in any prompt marks who is which. Most drama will
  come from the AI people, and the bots make a society for them to act in.
- **The same engine for both.** A person's mind (bot or AI) is a field on the person. A bot can
  hand over to an AI mind, for example when an AI person dies and a child inherits the slot.
  Capacity decides how many AI minds run.

## Architecture (`civ/`)

- **`content/`** is data.
  - Items: weight, worth, food, spoil, and hooks: tool uses, wear slot and warmth, weapon,
    armour, fuel, keep, carry, move, float, record.
  - Terrain, deposits, and wild and tame animals.
  - Buildings: cost, hours, hp and roles (shelter, store, workshop, farm, pen, wall, hearth,
    market, school, library, monument, grave, well, mill, road).
  - Crafts: era, workshop, prerequisites. Recipes: inputs, tools, hours, output, and whether it
    runs unattended.
  - A schema check proves every input can be obtained, every building built, and every era
    reached.
- **`world.py`** holds the state, with a spatial grid for sight and neighbour queries.
  **`gen.py`** makes the land: elevation and moisture give hills, forest, marsh, sand and rich
  soil; rivers run to a lake or sea; deposits are placed by geology; herds live where they live.
- **`engine.py`** is the tick loop over systems:
  - body: needs, ageing, warmth, sickness;
  - nature: regrowth, herds, predators, seasons, weather;
  - executor: runs intentions;
  - workshops: unattended processes;
  - knowledge: skill, teaching, watching;
  - society: deals, promises, service, posted trades, markets, groups, laws, treasuries;
  - conflict.
- **`plan.py`** is the recipe-tree planner: from what one holds, sees and remembers, it builds
  the steps to a goal. Bots use it for everything, and AI people can name a goal ("make a bronze
  axe") and have it laid out.
- **`minds/bot.py`** is the utility mind. **`minds/llm.py` and `prompt.py`** handle perception,
  intentions and the output schema.
- **`run.py`** and **`site.py`** run a world and build the viewer data. The gateway, the Kaggle
  pipeline and the logs are infrastructure and are reused.
