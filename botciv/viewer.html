Patterns

botciv is a small patch of land with too little food for everyone who lives on it. Each person's choices come from a language model that sees only what that person could see, remember and know. They are never told they are in a simulation.

A fixed set of rules decides what actually happens: food grows and spoils, hunts need company, winters are cold, people age, have children and die. Nothing social is scripted. Groups, deals, feuds, laws and rituals exist only if the people make them.

Each person is born with a temperament and one thing they want most in life. They keep their own notes and opinions of others, a few things they choose never to forget, and, if they change, a line on who they have become; the world keeps a true record of what passed between them. The chronicle is written from that record, and every sentence in it rests on events that were logged.
