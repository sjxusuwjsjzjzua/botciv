Speed

Speed is in hours of the world per second. Bubbles are what people said; dashed ones were whispered. Tap a person to read their life.

A world of people on a small patch of land, with too little food for everyone. Each person's choices come from a language model that sees only what that person could see, remember and know. They do not know they are in a simulation. A fixed set of rules decides what actually happens. Nothing social is scripted: groups, deals, feuds and laws exist only if the people make them.
